What the AI Act means for startups

Obligations for startups

Startups building high-risk AI systems face obligations around risk management, data governance, technical documentation and conformity assessment before deployment.

Regulatory sandboxes

Member states must offer regulatory sandboxes where a startup can test an AI system under supervision before full compliance obligations apply.

Penalties and proportionality

Penalties for SMEs and startups are applied proportionally, with the lower of the fixed amount or turnover percentage where specified.
