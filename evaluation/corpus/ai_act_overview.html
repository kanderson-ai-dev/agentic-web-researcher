The EU AI Act at a glance

Scope of the AI Act

The AI Act applies to providers and deployers of AI systems placed on the EU market, including general-purpose AI models. Its scope covers startups and enterprises alike.

Timeline

The regulation entered into force in August 2024. Obligations phase in over 6, 12, 24 and 36 months depending on the risk category.

Penalties

Non-compliance can lead to penalties of up to 35 million euros or 7% of global annual turnover for prohibited practices.
